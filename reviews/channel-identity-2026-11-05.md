full-bleed archival photographs, Photo Motion camera, geographic context and artifact details

Sound: bowed strings, breathy flute, frame drum and paper transitions. Each episode uses its retained subject fingerprint to vary the score.

The same original writer/reviewer/voice intents, topic ledger, post receipts and configured release dates remain authoritative. No existing film or post is regenerated. Owner approval refers to the visual direction, not a claim that the owner manually watched every daily episode. Automated technical tests cannot guarantee engagement or artistic quality.
